# Homework 1: Thinking Abstractly

## Question 1

**An algorithm is to be written to find the optimum route between two points.**

### (a) List three applications such an algorithm may be useful for`[3 marks]`

1.  Creating the fastest route (Google maps)
2.  Character movement in video games
3. Traffic control 

### (b) How has [[Abstraction|abstraction]] been used to reduce the complexity of these applications? `[3 marks]`

>In google maps unnecessary detail (such as trees) are removed, this means that the program can load and run faster as less elements are needed to be processed and it is easier for the users to operate as they only see the roads which is all they need for navigation.

## Question 3

**Police have known for a long time that everyone’s shoeprint is unique. In some areas of the UK, about 70% of suspects’ shoeprints are scanned and compared with the ones left at the scene of crime.**

### (a) Identify one similarity and one difference between fingerprint scanning and shoeprint scanning. `[2 marks]`

* **Similarity:** Can be used to identify someone
* **Difference:** Shoes can be removed

### (b) Give one reason why shoeprint scanning may be less effective than fingerprint scanning in catching criminals. `[1 mark]`

> After committing a crime suspects can destroy their shoes but its a lot harder for them to remove their fingerprints

### (c) Give one reason why shoeprint scanning may be more effective than fingerprint scanning in catching criminals. `[1 mark]`

> Its easier to spot shoeprints left behind at a crime scene  that it is to spot fingerprints
