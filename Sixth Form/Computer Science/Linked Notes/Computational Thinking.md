## What Is Computational Thinking?

> [!NOTE]
> Computational thinking is the ability to think logically about a problem and apply techniques for solving it.

Computational thinking is closely related to designing algorithms that can be turned into computer programs.

### Key Questions

- **What information is relevant to solving a particular problem?**
	You need to know what the problem you need to fix, what is needed to fix it and a rough plan of testing, Need to know what the problem needs to solve and what the fix needs to incorporate/extra features.
- **What computations need to be performed?**
	Decomposition --> (Breaking the problem down)
	Algorithmic thinking --> (Thinking in a structured and orderly way)
	Abstraction --> (Separating the logical and physical aspects of a problem)
- **How can we determine whether the problem has been solved?**
	Get the client to test it and iterate on needed features.
