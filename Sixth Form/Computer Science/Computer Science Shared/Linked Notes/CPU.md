CPU (Central Processing Unit) is the **main processor** of a computer and is responsible for processing instructions and controlling the computer's operations. It repeatedly **fetches, decodes and executes** instructions from [[Memory]], using components such as the **Control Unit (CU), Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU), registers and cache**.
