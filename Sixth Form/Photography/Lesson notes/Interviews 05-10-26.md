## Laura Lentinsky
#### Inspiration:
- 17 century Dutch still life paintings
- "women's work"
	- B genera - less important, inferior things  - the gathering together of everyday objects
- Things that people have a knee jerk rejections against
	- She wants to figure out why
- decaying objects
	- Bird skeletons and apple cores
- Still life
- Pulling things out of her image until it is on the threshold of breaking
- She puts  3D objects with 2D objects when making a collage
