## Destroy Identity
### Gut
To **destroy** means ==to damage or ruin something so badly that it no longer exists, works, or can be repaired==
*To gut* (verb): **To forcefully strip away or hollow out the core of a structure, leaving only an empty shell; to destroy from within.**
![[Destroy-Gut.jpg|670]]
To me, the verb 'to gut' has surgical connotation and leads me to image a more violent procedure as the action is forceful. I think metaphorically it presents the idea that something has been removed from a person, this person has then been *poorly* "repaired" and left worse off overall.
