#photograohy 
Rankin is a British photographer born in 1966, film director and publisher, he is known most for his campaigns working for brands among the likes of roles Royce and producing music videos for Miley Cyrus

As part of Rankins destroy project he photographed 70 musicians and asked them to "visually destroy" their own portraits, with the goal of hearing the voice inside that the camera does not normally catch. This allowed for the emotion of the subjects to be captured, with different musicians expressing a different method used to destroy themselves
