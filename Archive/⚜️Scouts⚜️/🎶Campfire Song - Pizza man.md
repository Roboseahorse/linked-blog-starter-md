#Scouts⚜️ 
# Campfire Songs: Hey Bo-Diddly Bop

> [!info] Performance Note
> This is a **repeat-after-me** song! The leader shouts a line, and the group repeats it back exactly.

---

## 🔁 The Chorus
Every verse begins with this standard repeating sequence:

> [!note] Chorus
> Hey bo-diddly bop
> I gotta get back to my block 

---
