#Scouts⚜️  
  

Create 2 "boats" by making 2 roped-off areas.

Put an equal number of bean bags (cannonballs) in each boat.

Divide the players into 2 teams, with one team in each boat.

When the game starts, try to throw all the cannonballs into the other team's boat.

The team with the most balls in their boat at the end of the game loses.

If someone who isn't playing shouts a special word, everyone has to stop and do a certain action.

For example:

 * Man overboard - jump out of the boat and into the water (you can also try to get balls (beanbags)  from the water)

 * Climb the rigging - do the "climb the rigging" action

 * Scrub the deck - get down and pretend to scrub the deck

  ---

## Potential risks
  

Injuries from bean bags: If thrown with force, bean bags could hurt someone.

Use soft bean bags: Choose bean bags that are soft and won't hurt if someone gets hit.

Slipping and falling: Wet or slippery surfaces from water can cause falls.

Safe playing area: Make sure the playing area is clear of obstacles and hazards. 

Overcrowding: If too many people play in a small space, it can be unsafe.

Choose a safe space: Make sure the area is big enough for everyone to play comfortably.

Take turns: Everyone should have a chance to play.
