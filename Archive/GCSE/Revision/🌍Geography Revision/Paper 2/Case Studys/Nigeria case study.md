# Case Studies

## Nigeria
Nigeria is a **west African** country which has experienced recent development to become an **emerging country**. It is found in the west of Africa and is having a large **positive influence** on the world.

### Nigeria's influence:
- Nigeria has the **highest GDP in Africa**, and has the **27th largest economy** in the world — larger than Norway, Greece and the UAE
- Nigeria has a large supply of **oil**, which they sell to the world. Countries that supply oil have large **political influence** over those who don't
- Nigeria has the **7th largest population** in the world, with **200 million** Nigerians — three times bigger than the UK's population

### What about the rest of Nigeria?

| Nigeria's Culture | Politics | Life in Nigeria |
|---|---|---|
| Nigeria has the **second largest film industry** in the world, nicknamed ***Nollywood*** | Nigeria has contributed military assistance to many **UN peacekeeping tours**, especially protecting a ceasefire in Liberia | Over **70% of the population** are farmers, so Nigeria relies predominantly on **agriculture & farming** |
| Nigeria is socially diverse, with many **ethnic groups** and religions — a result of its many tribes and being ruled by Britain during the **British Empire** | However, Nigeria has battled to stop its **corrupt government**. Much of the population has lost trust in the government, who accepts money bribes | Nigeria has a variety of **landscapes**, from hot desert to tropical rainforests, meaning many different **crops** are grown and living conditions vary greatly |

## Nigeria's Environment & Lifestyles
Nigeria's wealth has been **increasing** over the last century. However, a recent **recession** has resulted in **unemployment** and **slow improvements** in the lives of Nigerians.

| Measure | Nigeria's Figure |
|---|---|
| **Life Expectancy** | **54 years** — the world average is 73 years, significantly higher than Nigeria's |
| **Literacy Rates** | **65.1%** — very low, especially in girls due to inequality in aspirations. Only one in three children stay in school |
| **Human Development Index** | **158 out of 188** — low life expectancy and large inequality in wealth has resulted in this low rank |

## Industry
Nigeria has a **large agriculture industry**, despite being a developing country. Agriculture has the largest number of workers, many of whom practice **subsistence farming** — growing just enough food to feed themselves and their family. This is unusual for a developing country, as farmers don't make much profit.

Most developing countries have large **manufacturing industries** because manufactured goods make more profit than raw materials. In Nigeria, manufacturing is mainly in the form of **processing oil** that has been mined. Oil makes a **large profit** and gives Nigeria **political influence** over other countries.

### TNCs
The most influential **TNCs** in Nigeria are **oil companies**:
- Foreign companies such as French company **Total** and the USA's **Exxon-Mobil** invest in Nigeria and buy rights to export oil from the country
- Nigeria has its own TNC — **Transcorp** — which provides hospitality services across the globe, as well as energy

> [!success] Advantages of Foreign TNCs
> - Foreign oil TNCs can **invest** in new production and mining sites, providing **more jobs** for Nigerians
> - TNCs **share technology** across countries — for example, new safety measures or mining practices developed elsewhere can be shared with Nigerian oil workers

> [!danger] Disadvantages of Foreign TNCs
> - Oil is very damaging to the **environment**. Oil spills damage habitats and harm wildlife — the **Niger river** has regular oil spills, affecting wildlife and local residents' health
> - Burning fossil fuels like oil contributes to **Global Warming**, which directly impacts Nigeria through droughts, desertification, increased flooding and extreme weather

## Aid
There have been several **NGO campaigns** in Nigeria to improve the quality of life and health of locals. This is the focus of most aid, as Nigeria has a large economy but much of its wealth is made by rich oil companies and is not shared with Nigeria's poorer residents.

| Christian Aid | Action Aid |
|---|---|
| Protecting **displaced Nigerians** who have had to leave their homes due to violence in North Nigeria | Encouraging **girls to go to school** by constructing girls' toilets and providing sanitation products, so they don't miss school because of their period |
| Supporting individuals with **HIV** in support groups, to reduce discrimination in the community | During **COVID-19**, delivered food parcels to families who could no longer afford food due to job losses |
| Medical treatment to **200,000 children under 5** for Malaria, Pneumonia and Diarrhoea | Pressuring **TNCs to pay their taxes** in Nigeria, so the government can invest in communities |
