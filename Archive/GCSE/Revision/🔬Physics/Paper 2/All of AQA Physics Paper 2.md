## Vectors & Scalars
**Contact Force**: When objects physically touching
**Non-Contact force**: When not touching e.g. magnetism, electrostatic force & gravity

A **Scalar** is a quantity that only has MAGNITUDE
A **Vector** is a quantity that has MAGNITUDE & DIRECTION

If there are two forces acting on an object there is a resultant force
If the forces are at right angles from each other you use Pythagoras to find the resultant
If resultant force is *0N* then the object is moving at a constant speed

| SCALARS      | VECTORS          |
| ------------ | ---------------- |
| **Distance** | **Displacement** |
| *Speed*        | *Velocity*         |
| mass         | Acceleration     |
| energy       | force            |

## Springs & Hooke's law
Any object that deform ELASTICLLY (returns to its original shape) obeys HOOKE'S LAW: (force and extension are DIRECTLY PROPORTIONAL) 
### Practical
- Add masses onto spring to change force (e.g. 100g =1N)
- Measure extension with ruler at each weight, plot F against e.
- Gradient of graph = spring constant

## Moments
**Principle of moments:** When the SUM of CLOCKWISE MOMENTS = SUM of ANTICLOCKWISE MOMENTS a system is in EQULIBRUIM (it will not turn)
**Gears** can be used to increase moments by driving a larger gear with a smaller one.

## Pressure
**Pressure underwater** is due to the weight of the column of water above an object pushing down on it
**Pressure in gas** is due to the particles colliding with the walls of its container, exertinfg a force on them. To increase this: DECREASE VOLUME, ADD MORE HAS (more frequent collisions), or INCREASE TEMPRATURE (more frequent collusion due to faster speed, AND collide with more momentum, so greater force)
