## Size of force depends on:
1. mass of objects
   Amount of matter in object
2. How far apart objects are

For objects that have small mass the gravitational force of attraction is so Tiny is like it doesn't exist

For very large objects that are also relatively close by, such as the earth or the moon, gravity is felt far more strongly and have a big influence over nearby objects. We call this field of influence its gravitational field, the strength of this field is called gravitational field strength which is shown with the letter (g). For earth the gravitational field strength is around 9.8 N/kg

## Calculations:
Weight=Mass * gravitational field strength

==Weight= force exerted on an object due to gravity:== 
Weight Depends on:
1. mass of object
2. Gravitational pull

## Gravitational potential energy:
**Formula**~~ E<sub>p</sub>= mgh
Gravitational potential energy(J)= mass(kg) * gravitational field strength(N/Kg) * height(m)
