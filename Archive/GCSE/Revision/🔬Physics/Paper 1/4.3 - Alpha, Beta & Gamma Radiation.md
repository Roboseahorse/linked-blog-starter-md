Each element comes in a n umber of different forms which have the same number of protons but a different number of neutrons, only one or two of an elements isotopes are stable while the rest are unstable
	If an isotope is unstable it can undergo radioactive decay, This is where the isotope emits something so that they can change slightly and become more stable. Therefore when we say a material is radioactive all we mean is that it consists of unstable isotopes that can decay

**Alpha particles:**

Alpha particles are made up of ==2 protons and two neutron== This is the same as the nucleus of a Helium atom so alpha is usually represented with Helium's nuclear symbol (He)
Alpha particles DO NOT have any electrons so they have an overall charge of 2+ (They get this from the two protons that they have)
Alpha particles are relatively large:
	They are easily stopped by colliding with other molecules
		This means they can't penetrate very far into other materials
	They can only travel a few cm In air 
	and they are absorbed by single sheets of paper
Their large size and strong charge makes them verry strongly ionising, *which means they can easily nock electrons off any atom they collide with* 

**Beta Particles:**

Just electrons, They have a charge of -1 and virtually no mass.
Electrons aren't emitted from one of the atoms shells, instead one of the atoms neutrons decays into a proton and an electron, The proton stays in the nucleus but the electron is emitted out at high speed.
Because Beta particles are pretty Tiny they are moderately Ionising, and the penetrate moderately far into materials
 It will take ==several meters of air== **OR** ==5mm of aluminium== to stop them ^79a2ab

**Gamma Rays:**

Gamma rays actually aren't particles at all they are instead ==waves of electromagnetic radiation== and is often emitted after alpha of beta radiation as a way for the nucleus to get rid of a bit of extra energy. 
As they don't have any mass or any charge they tend to pass straight through materials rather then colliding with any of the atoms.  ^01985e
 - ==This makes them weekly ionising== 
 - Additionally this means that they can penetrate really far into materials without being stopped.
 - It takes thick sheets of lead/multiple meters of concrete to stop them.

**Emission of a Neutron:**

If a nucleus contains too many neutrons which is making it unstable they can through out a neutron to increase its stability
