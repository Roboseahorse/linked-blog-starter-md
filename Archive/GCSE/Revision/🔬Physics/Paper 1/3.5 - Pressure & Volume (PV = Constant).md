Pressure in gasses is caused by particles colliding with the walls of the container
Pressure can be increased by:
	- Increasing temperature
	- Increasing the number of particles
	- Decreasing volume
	  This means that pressure and volume are inversely related, so as one goes up the other goes down

Pressure * volume = Constant
**OR**
Pressure(1)*Volume(1) = Pressure(2)*Volume(2)
