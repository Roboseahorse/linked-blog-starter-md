## Temperature
If temperature increases the resistance would also increase
And in practise a large current can cause the wires to heat up

## Filament laps
Filament lamps contain thin metal filaments, as the current flows through the filament the wire heats up until its so hot that it releases light, this heat massively increases resistance and the curve gets less steep as the current increases. which shows that less current can flow per unit of [[2.2 - V = IR Equation & I-V Graphs#^Potentialdifference|Potential difference]] 

## Diode
- Only allow current to flow in 1 direction 
	They do this by having really high resistance in the reverse direction
- So on a IV graph the current is only showed when V is positive
