## Reaction rates using moles
- The rate of reaction is a measure of changing property per unit of time
- There is no direct way to measure moles, so first it is necessary to [[Calculating rate of reaction|calculate the rate]] 

Moles per unit time = Mass per unit time ÷ molar mass of the substance

---
## Calculation example

20.0 g of marble chips were added to a flask containing 100 cm 3 of 2.0 mol/dm 3 hydrochloric acid. The flask was placed on a balance and the mass was measured at the start of the reaction. After 2.5 minutes the mass was found to have decreased by 6.0 g.

Calculate the rates of reaction, in mol/s.

**Answer:**

- **Step 1:** Convert the time into seconds:
    

2.5 x 60 = 150 s

- **Step 2:** Convert the mass lost to moles:
    
    - The mass loss is due to carbon dioxide _(M_ r = 44.0) being released
