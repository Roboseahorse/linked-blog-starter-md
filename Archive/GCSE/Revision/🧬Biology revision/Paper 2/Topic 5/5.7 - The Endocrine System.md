The **endocrine system** allows different parts of our body to **communicate** with each other
The **Endocrine System** consists of a series of glands spread throughout the body, what they have in common is that all the glands **secrete hormones** which are small chemical molecules that that are passed into the blood and then spread through the body in the blood stream, This allows the **hormones** to come into contact with a **large range of tissues**, some of these tissues have **receptors** that are specific to **hormones**, that **trigger changes inside the cell** 

## Glands
#### Pituitary gland:
Sometimes called the **master gland**, it produces **multiple different hormones**; some of which directly *tell the body what to do* and some which tell **other glands** to **release their own hormones**. Its **NOT** a prat of the [[5.3 - The Brain|Brain]] but it is pretty close and actually attached to the [[5.3 - The Brain|Brain]] 
#### Thyroid gland:
Located in the neck and produces the hormone **[[5.16- Adrenaline & Thyroxine|Thyroxine]]** 
	**Thyroxine** regulates the rate of metabolism and also plays an important role in growth and development
If the [[5.7 - The Endocrine System#Pituitary gland|Pituitary gland]] detects low levels of Thyroxine, [[5.7 - The Endocrine System#Pituitary gland|Pituitary gland]] releases **TSH, Thyroid stimulating hormone** which stimulates the **thyroid** to make more thyroxine, This process is called **negative feedback** 

#### Adrenal Gland:
Sit just on top of our kidneys and produce [[5.16- Adrenaline & Thyroxine|Adrenaline]]
**, this is normal released during the **flight or flight response** to get us ready for fight or run by doing things like increasing our heart rate and getting out blood pumping harder

#### Pancreas
Just Infront of the kidneys we have the pancreas, which produces the hormone **insulin** which regulates our **blood glucose concentration**

#### Testes
Only found in males and produces the hormone testosterone, also produces the male gametes sperm

#### Ovaries
Only found in females, there main goal is to produce oestrogen which influences puberty and the menstrual cycle

## Endocrine system
1. relies on **hormones**, which are small molecules produced in the glands and **transported by blood**
2. Spread more slowly and their effects last longer
3. Act more generally as hormones spread through the body and interact with lots of different cells
