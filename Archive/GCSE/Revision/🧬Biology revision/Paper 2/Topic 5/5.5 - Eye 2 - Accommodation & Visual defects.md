## Accommodation
- A reflex that changes the refractive power of the [[5.4  - Eye 1 - Structure of the eye and iris reflex#Lens|lens]], so that you can see both near and distance objects
- Whenever light hits the eye, it has to be focused to the [[5.4  - Eye 1 - Structure of the eye and iris reflex#Fovea|fovea]] at the back of the eye, this is mainly achieved by the [[5.4  - Eye 1 - Structure of the eye and iris reflex#Cornea|cornea]] which always refracts light by the same amount. While the lens acts as a fine tune for refraction

A close object has to be refracted a lot so even after it has been refracted by the cornea, it needs the even more powerful **lens** to refract it soe more. To be able to refract strongly the lens needs to be short and fat
