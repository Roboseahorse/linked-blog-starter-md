## Nerve cells
Adapted to carry **electrical** impulses from one point to another
- They are long & thin
- They have branched connections at either end
	- This allows them to pass messages
### Synapse
- connections between cells
- Whenever an electrical impulse reaches the end of a nerve, it causes the release of chemicals which diffuse across the gap to the next nerve cell 
- where they can triggered another electrical impulse

- Takes place sensory information

## Reflex arc
Technical term for the nerve pathway that underlies or unconscious reflexes
	Such as moving you hand away from a hot pan
The benefit of having these reflexes is that they are rapid and automatic so we can respond to things really quickly
1. The stimuli is detected by the receptor cells in skin
2. the receptor cells stimulate sensory neurones that carries an impulse up to the the spinal cord ^6f65fe
3. The spinal cord transfers the impulse to the relay neurone
4. The relay neurone will then pass the impulse along to a motor neurone 
5. where it travels back out to the body to an effector such as a bicep
6. which will contract to move your hand away
Between each of the different neurones there are synapses where the electrical impulse is temporarily turned into a chemical signal so it can be passed between the different nerve cells
