### Cerebral Cortex
The part that is responsible for:
- Consciousness 
- intelligence
- memory
- language and hearing
The cerebral cortex is split into two parts that we call **'hemispheres'**, the **left hemisphere** controls the muscles in the **right side of the body**, and the **right hemisphere** controls the muscles on the **left side of the body**
### Cerebellum
The part that is responsible for:
- balance 
- muscle coordination
### Hypothalamus
This part is responsible for:
- regulating body temperature
- sends signals to the **[[5.7 - The Endocrine System#Pituitary gland|Pituitary Gland]]**
### Brain stem
- Connects brain to the spinal cord
#### Medulla
Controls unconscious activities:
(Breathing)(beating of our hearts)

## How scientists study the brain
#### 1. Study people with brain damage:
- If someone has had a stroke, in which a tiny part of their brain is damaged, and it leads to them not being able to hear any more, then we can make a pretty good guess that, the damaged part of the brain is responsible for hearing in some way
#### 2. Electrically stimulate different parts of the brain
- If we zap one part of the brain with a tiny electrode it could result in muscle contraction and the person might move an arm or a leg
#### 3. Scanning the brain
- **CT Scan** which uses ([[4.3 - Alpha, Beta & Gamma Radiation|X-rays]]) 
	 *useful for seeing which areas of the brain are damaged*
- **PET scan** which uses radioactive chemicals
	*measuring the underlying activity of the brain*
- **MRI scan** which use really strong magnetic fields
	*measuring the underlying activity of the brain*


## Why treating the brain is so difficult
- There is a wide range of things that can go wrong, including:
	Tumours
	Truman
	Mental health problems
	Infection
- The fact that it is encased in skull and that the surrounding brain tissue is fragile, makes it difficult to physically fix anything with surgery 
- Because the brain is so complex and we don't fully understand it it is verry difficult to fix it with drugs and chemicals
