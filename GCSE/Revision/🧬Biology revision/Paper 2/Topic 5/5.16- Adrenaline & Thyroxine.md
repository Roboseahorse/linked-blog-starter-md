## Adrenaline
- Adrenaline is a hormone produced by the adrenal glands ( which sit just on top of the kidneys)
- Adrenaline is produced during the '**fight or flight** response', when you get **scared**, **stressed** or need to exercise. it prepares the body for activity 
#### What does adrenaline do?
1. Increases heart rate
2. Increases **blood pressure**
3. Increase **blood flow** to muscles
4. Increases **blood sugar (glucose) levels** by stimulating the liver to break down glycogen down into glucose

## Thyroxine
- Thyroxine is produced by the [[5.7 - The Endocrine System#Thyroid gland|thyroid gland]], which is found in the neck.
- Thyroxine has lots of toles in the body, including growth and development, but the main tole is to **increase your metabolic rate** (the rate at which chemical reactions are taking place in your body)
#### Production of Thyroxine
1. **If thyroxine levels are too low** - the [[5.7 - The Endocrine System#Pituitary gland|Pituitary gland]] will release **TSH (Thyroid stimulating hormone)**. This then **stimulates the thyroid gland to release more thyroxine**. So thyroxine levels in the blood increase back up to normal.
2. **If thyroxine levels are too high** - the thyroxine will inhibit the [[5.7 - The Endocrine System#Pituitary gland|Pituitary gland]] from producing **TSH**. Less **TSH** means that the **thyroid gland won't release as much thyroxine**. So thyroxine levels in the blood fall back to normal
The control of thyroxine levels by the hypothalamus and pituitary gland is an example of **negative feedback**. If levels of thyroxine rise too high, it will bring about changes to lower the level, and if they fall too low, it brings about changed to raise them back up
