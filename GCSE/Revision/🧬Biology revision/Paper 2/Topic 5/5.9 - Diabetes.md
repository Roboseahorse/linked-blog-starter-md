## Insulin
- Hormone released by the pancreas when the concentration of glucose in the blood stream gets to high.
- Insulin binds to cells through the body and tells he cells to absorb more glucose and turn it into glycogen

## Type 1 Diabetes
- Developed in childhood or teenage years
- lifelong condition
- for some reason the pancreas stops producing insulin (or makes tiny amounts)
#### Treatment
- Inject insulin after a meal:
	- into abdomen or thigh
- This decreases blood glucose concentration before they get too high
- Amount of insulin that they need to inject depends on two things:
	- What they've eaten
		- Lots of carbohydrates --> lots of insulin needed
	- How much exercise they do
		- More exercise would mean they need less insulin

## Type 2 Diabetes
- developed in older people who have has unhealthy diets for a long time
- The persons cells become resistant to insulin, meaning they don't respond to it any more, this means they wont absorb glucose from the blood stream
####  Main treatment
- Healthy, low-sugar diet
- Regular execrise
