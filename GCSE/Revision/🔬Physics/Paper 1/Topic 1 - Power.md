## Power & the The Watt

- Machines such as car engines, transfer energy from one energy store to another constantly over a period of **time**
- The rate of this energy transfer, or the rate of work done, is called power
- Two cars transfer the **same amount of [[1.1 - Energy Stores & Systems|energy]]**, or do the **same amount of work** to accelerate over a distance
	- if one of the car has **more power**, it will transfer that energy, or do that work, in a **shorter amount of time
Power is defined as **Energy transferred per unit time*

Power is measured in **watts** where one watt is equivalent to **one joule per second**

**1W=J/S**

1 kilowatt *(1kW)* is equal to 1000 watts, or 1000 joules of energy transferred per seconds *(1kJ/s)*

---

## Power Calculations

Power can be calculated using the equation:

P=E/t

Where:

P= Power in **watts** (W)

E= energy transferred in **joules** (J)

t= time in **seconds** (s)

Since energy transferred = work done, in the equation energy transferred can also be swapped out for (w)**Work done** in **joules**(J)
