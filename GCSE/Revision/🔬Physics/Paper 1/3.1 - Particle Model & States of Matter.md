Particle theory (kinetic Theory) - Explain how the particles in each state behave, by considering each of the particles as a small solid inelastic shear.

## **Solid:**
Strong forces of attraction between the particles, which hold them all together in a fixed position, because the substance is fixed the overall substance keeps a definite shape and [[3.5 - Pressure & Volume (PV = Constant)#^319b6d|volume]] (so it cant flow like a liquid). Each particle in a solid vibrates in a fixed position. 
	*Heating up a solid:*
		Gain more energy (vibrate more)
		weakens the forces of attraction between the particles
	*Melting point*
		Particles have enough energy to break free of their bonds and so a solid melts into a liquid

## **Liquid:**
Only weak forces of attraction between the particles
		Meaning the particles are free to move around
		Means particles tend to stick together and are compact (Have a definite volume although their overall shape may change) allowing them to flow to fit a certain container
*Heating up a liquid:*
		The particles gain more energy (the particles move around faster)
		This weakens the forces of attraction between the particles
*Boiling point:*
		Particles will have enough energy to break the bonds all together so the liquid breaks or evaporates into a gas
*cooling down liquids:*
	The liquids wont have enough energy to overcome the attraction so they will start forming even more bonds, his freezes a liquid into a solid, and fixing the particles in place

## **Gas:**
Forces of attraction are very weak so the particles are basically free to move around themselves, this means particles don't keep a definite shape or [[3.5 - Pressure & Volume (PV = Constant)#^319b6d|volume]] and always fill a container as they spread out as much a possible. We say that gasses are constantly moving with a *Radom motion* (The particles can travel in any direction)
*Heating up a gas:*
	When heating up a gas the particles get more energy and travel faster
When the particles in a gas travel faster they will either expand if the container they are in is expandable (like a balloon) or if the container if fixed the [[3.5 - Pressure & Volume (PV = Constant)|Pressure]] will increase
*Cooling down gases:*
	The gases wont have enough energy to overcome force of attraction so they will start forming bonds, this condenses a gas into a liquid
