==Kinetic Energy= Energy possessed due to motion== 
The amount of kinetic energy the object has depends on two things:
	Its speed
		For speed the faster an object is moving the more kinetic energy it will have 
	Its mass
		as long as everything else remains equal (for example speed) then the more mass it has the more kinetic energy it has

## Calculation:
E<sub>k</sub> =1/2 mv<sup>2</sup> 
E=energy
<sub>K</sub> = Tells us that it is kinetic energy
M=Mass(kg)
V= Velocity(speed in a given direction)
