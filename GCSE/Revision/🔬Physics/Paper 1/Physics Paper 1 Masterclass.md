## Pressure exam Q

The temperature of the helium in the balloon was increased.

The mass and volume in the helium balloon remained constant.

Explain why the [[3.5 - Pressure & Volume (PV = Constant)|pressure]] exerted by the helium inside the balloon would increase.

- more energy in helium particles in kinetic store
- Particles move faster
- Collisions occur more **frequently** between the helium particles and the container
- Therefore is a **greater force** exerted in the collisions
- Therefore pressure increases due to the **greater force exerted on the same area**

## [[2.12 - static Electricity|Static Electricity]] 

- some insulating materials can become electrically charged when they are rubbed together
- The material that **gains electrons** will become **negatively charged**
- The material that **loses electrons** will become **positively charged**
