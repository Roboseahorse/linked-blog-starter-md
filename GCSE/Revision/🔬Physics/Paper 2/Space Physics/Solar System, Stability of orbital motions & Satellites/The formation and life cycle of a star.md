All stars begin life in the same way. A cloud of dust and gas, also knows as a, [[The formation and life cycle of a star#^Nebula|Nebula]], becomes a protostar, which goes on to become a [[The formation and life cycle of a star#^mainSquence|main sequence]] star. Following this, stars develop in different ways depending on their size.


#### Definitions
**Nebula**: A cloud of has and dust in outer space. If massive enough, these can collapse under gravity to from a protostar ^Nebula

**Main sequence**: A stable stage in the life cycle of a star. Nuclear fusion occurs, fusing hydrogen nuclei into helium nuclei. There is a balance between the outwards radiation and the force of gravity pulling inwards. ^mainSquence
